# Scrutiny Officer Manual

**Non-Teaching Recruitment Portal, ABV-IIITM Gwalior**

This manual is for officers who scrutinise submitted applications. Most scrutiny officers hold the **Scrutiny User** role; Recruitment Administrators and the Super Administrator can also be assigned to a scrutiny stage and use the same screens.

---

## 1. Getting access

1. The Super Administrator creates your staff account. You receive an email with a link to set your password. If it has expired, ask for **Send password reset link** to be used again.
2. Sign in at the portal's **Sign in** page. Scrutiny Users land on **My scrutiny queue**.
3. You may also be placed in a **scrutiny team** for an advertisement or post. Team members can open and read that team's applications, but you can **record verdicts and reports only on applications assigned to you**, at the stage assigned to you.

## 2. Scrutiny at a glance

1. The Recruitment Section assigns an application to you for a **stage** (for example "Initial scrutiny"). You receive an email "Application assigned for …" and a notification.
2. Open it from **My scrutiny queue** and click **Scrutinise**.
3. Record a verdict on each checklist item, using the documents and the advisory eligibility checks.
4. Add observations and, if needed, request a clarification from the candidate.
5. Submit your **Stage report**. The application moves to the next stage.
6. At the final **decision stage**, the assigned officer records the outcome.

Scrutiny never changes what the candidate submitted. Verdicts, observations and clarifications are recorded separately.

---

## 3. Find your work

**My scrutiny queue** has two tabs:

- **Open** — applications assigned to you that you have not yet reported on. Click **Scrutinise**.
- **Submitted** — applications on which you have submitted a stage report. Click **View**.

Columns show **Reference**, **Candidate**, **Post / Advt.**, **Stage** (a "decision" tag marks the decision stage), **Status**, **Fee / Referees** and **Assigned** date. Use **Filter this page…** to search.

## 4. The scrutiny workspace

The header shows the candidate, reference number, post, advertisement, category, your stage and the scrutiny status. A progress line shows "x of y items recorded", mandatory items still pending, and the fee, documents and referee status.

Banners you may see:

- "The candidate has reopened this application; you are reviewing the last submitted version."
- A grey lock message if you can read but not act (see [Troubleshooting](#11-troubleshooting)).

The left side holds the **Scrutiny checklist** and the **Stage report**. The right side has tabs: **Application**, **Documents**, **Referees**, **Eligibility**, **Observations**, **Clarifications** and **Stage reports**.

The **Scrutiny report PDF** button downloads a report of the checklist, eligibility, observations, clarifications and stage reports. Each download is logged.

## 5. Record a checklist verdict

1. In **Scrutiny checklist**, use the filter (**All items**, **Pending**, **Not verified**, **Mandatory**) to find items.
2. Each item shows its label, whether it is **Mandatory**, what the **Candidate marked** (✓ Enclosed, X Not enclosed, NA or Not marked) with any page reference, verification instructions, and the linked **Supporting document**. Click the document to open it in a new tab.
3. Choose a verdict: **Verified**, **Not verified**, **Not applicable** or **Pending**.
4. For **Not verified**, enter the **Discrepancy reason** (required where the item demands a reason).
5. Optionally add **Remarks** and choose **Document verified against**.
6. Click **Save** (or **Undo** to discard). The item then shows "Recorded by [your name], [date and time]".

You can change a verdict until you submit your stage report. Every change is recorded in the audit trail.

## 6. Verify a document

If your role includes document verification (Scrutiny Users have it by default):

1. Open the **Documents** tab. Click **Open** or **Download** to see a file. Every view and download is logged.
2. Click **Verify** and choose **Verified**, **Deficient** (remarks required) or **Pending**, then **Save**.
3. The application's overall document status becomes **Deficient** if any document is deficient, and **Verified** once all are verified.

The file is never altered by verification.

## 7. Use the eligibility checks

The **Eligibility** tab compares what the candidate **declared** with the criteria set for the post, for example minimum percentage, experience, maximum age or qualification level. Each result is **Met**, **Not met** or **Verify manually**, with the basis (for example "Age 31 yr 4 mo on 30-11-2026; limit 30 years — check applicable relaxation").

These results are **advisory only**. They never change the application's status. Always verify against the documents and apply relaxations as per the advertisement.

## 8. Record observations

1. Open **Observations**.
2. Under **Add observation** choose a **Section** (General or a form section), tick **This is a deficiency in the application** if it is one, write the **Observation**, and click **Add observation**.
3. Click **Resolve** when an observation has been dealt with.

Unresolved deficiencies, together with "Not verified" checklist items, appear as **Outstanding deficiencies** in the scrutiny report seen by the Screening Committee.

## 9. Request a clarification from the candidate

You need the permission to send clarification requests (Scrutiny Users have it by default).

1. Open **Clarifications** and click **Request clarification**.
2. Enter **Subject** and **Message**.
3. Set **Respond by**, or leave it blank for the default period set by the Recruitment Section.
4. Tick **Allow the candidate to upload documents with the response** if needed (the default follows the advertisement's setting).
5. Optionally tick **Related checklist items**.
6. Click **Send request**.

What happens next:

- The candidate is emailed and sees the request on their application page. Scrutiny status becomes **Clarification required**.
- When the candidate responds, you get a notification, the status becomes **Clarification received**, and the response and any documents appear under the request.
- Click **Mark resolved** or **Close**, choose **Resolved** or **Closed without resolution**, add **Remarks**, and **Save**. When no request remains open, the status returns to **In progress**.
- After the due date the candidate can no longer respond online.

## 10. Submit your stage report

### Ordinary (non-decision) stage

1. Make sure every **mandatory** checklist item has a verdict other than Pending.
2. In the **Stage report** box choose **Completeness**: **Complete**, **Incomplete** or **Clarification required**.
3. Choose **Recommendation**: **Recommended for further consideration** or **Recommended for rejection**.
4. Enter **Reasons** (required if you recommend rejection or the application is not complete) and an optional **Summary of scrutiny**.
5. Any problem is listed above the button (for example "2 mandatory checklist item(s) still pending."). If a clarification request is still open, you must either resolve it or choose **Clarification required**.
6. Click **Submit stage report…**, check the summary, and click **Confirm and submit**.

Once submitted, your report is locked and the application moves to the next stage. The Recruitment Section is emailed with your recommendation. Scrutiny status shows **Completed** until the next stage begins.

### Decision stage

1. Choose a **Decision**: **Recommended (complete)**, **Incomplete**, **Not recommended**, or **Return to previous stage**.
2. **Reasons** are required for every decision except Recommended.
3. Tick **Notify the candidate of this decision by email** if the candidate should be told. If you do not tick it, no decision email is sent.
4. Click **Record decision…** and **Confirm and submit**.

The decision becomes the **Scrutiny outcome** and the scrutiny status becomes **Decided**. Only applications with the outcome **Recommended** can normally be forwarded to the Screening Committee.

**Return to previous stage** sends the application back to the previous stage's officer. Their report reopens, marked **Returned for revision**, and the application reappears in their **Open** queue. The decision is recorded again after they resubmit.

### Separation of duties

Unless the Recruitment Section has allowed the same reviewer for a stage, an officer who reported on an earlier stage of an application cannot be assigned to, or report on, a later stage of the same application.

## 11. Troubleshooting

The grey lock banner explains why you cannot act:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "This application has not yet been placed in a scrutiny stage." | It has not been assigned yet. |
| "You have already submitted your report for '…'. The workspace is read-only." | Your part is done. Ask the Recruitment Section if a change is needed; the decision stage can return it to you. |
| "You are not assigned to the current stage ('…') of this application. You can read it but not record verdicts." | Another officer holds the current stage, or you were never assigned. Ask the Recruitment Section to assign you. |
| "Your stage report has been submitted; the application is now at '…', which is not assigned to you." | The application has moved on. |
| "Your account does not hold the scrutiny permission for this application." | Ask the Super Administrator to check your role's permissions. |

Other messages:

- **"Record a verdict on every mandatory checklist item: …"** — complete the listed items first.
- **"A clarification request is still open. Resolve it or report the application as requiring clarification."** — resolve or close the request, or choose **Clarification required**.
- **"This user reported on an earlier stage of this application…"** — separation of duties; another officer must be assigned.
- **Supporting-document buttons on checklist items are greyed out ("You do not have permission to open documents") and the Documents tab has no Open / Download buttons** — your role lacks document access; contact the Super Administrator.
- **No Request clarification button** — your role lacks the clarification permission.
- **An application disappeared from my Open list** — it was reassigned to another officer, or you have submitted your report (see **Submitted**).

## 12. Status glossary

| Item | Values |
|---|---|
| Your assignment | Assigned · In progress · Submitted · Withdrawn (reassigned to someone else) |
| Scrutiny status | Submitted · Received · Assigned · In progress · Clarification required · Clarification received · Completed · Under review · Decided |
| Checklist verdict | Verified · Not verified · Not applicable · Pending |
| Document verification | Pending · Verified · Deficient (shown as "Rejected" in some lists) |
| Eligibility check | Met · Not met · Verify manually |
| Clarification | Open · Responded · Resolved · Closed |
| Scrutiny outcome | Recommended · Incomplete · Not recommended |

## 13. Confidentiality

- The **Referees** tab shows each referee's email delivery, recommendation status and deadline. It does **not** show what referees wrote.
- Recommendation content is visible only to staff whose role includes reading confidential recommendations. By default Scrutiny Users do not have this. If your role has been given it, a "Recommendations received (confidential)" list appears, and every recommendation you open is logged with your name and the time.
- Referee recommendations must never be disclosed to the candidate.
- Every document you open or download, and every scrutiny report PDF, is recorded in the document access log.
- Keep downloaded files secure and delete local copies when scrutiny is complete, in line with the Institute's rules.
