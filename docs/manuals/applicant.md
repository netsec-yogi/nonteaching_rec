# Applicant Manual

**Non-Teaching Recruitment Portal, ABV-IIITM Gwalior**

This manual is for anyone applying for a non-teaching post at ABV-IIITM Gwalior through the online portal. It follows the numbering of the paper application form (Sections 1 to 32 and the Check List), so you can match each online step to the printed form.

---

## 1. Before you begin

You will need:

- An email address and an Indian mobile number that you check regularly. All messages about your application go to this email address.
- Scanned copies of your certificates and documents, saved as **PDF**, **JPEG** or **PNG** files (see [Uploading documents](#5-uploading-documents)).
- The details of your fee payment (NEFT UTR number or Demand Draft number), unless you are exempt from the fee.
- The names, addresses, phone numbers and **email addresses** of your referees (usually three; the advertisement tells you how many).
- If you are in service: the endorsement from your employer, if the advertisement asks for it.

## 2. The process at a glance

1. **Register** and verify your email address.
2. Open an advertisement, read it, and click **Apply for this post**.
3. Fill in the 17 steps of the online form. Your work is saved step by step, so you can stop and return.
4. Open **Preview & submit**, check everything and click **Submit application**.
5. You receive a **reference number**, an email with a PDF copy of your application, and an **Acknowledgment receipt** you can download.
6. Your referees are emailed automatically and send their confidential recommendations directly to the Institute.
7. The Recruitment Section verifies your fee and documents, and scrutinises the application. They may ask you for a **clarification**, or **reopen** the application so you can correct it.
8. You can follow progress from **My Applications** at any time.

## 3. Your account

### Create an account

1. On the home page click **Register** (or **Register to apply**).
2. Fill in **Full name** (as it appears in your matriculation certificate), **Email address**, **Mobile number**, **Password** and **Confirm password**.
3. Your password must have at least 10 characters, upper and lower case letters, at least one number and at least one symbol (for example @ # $ %). The ticks below the password box turn green as each rule is met.
4. Tick the box agreeing to the privacy notice and click **Create account**.

One account is enough for any number of posts.

### Verify your email address

1. Open the email titled "Verify your email address" and click **Verify my email address**.
2. The link is valid for 48 hours.
3. You may start filling in an application before verifying, but **you cannot submit until your email is verified**.
4. If the email has not arrived, click **Resend email** (on My Applications) or **Resend verification email** (on the Account page or the Preview page).

### Sign in and sign out

1. Click **Sign in**, enter your email address and password, and click **Sign in**.
2. Do not tick **Keep me signed in on this device** on a shared or public computer.
3. After five wrong passwords in a row your account is locked for 15 minutes.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and click **Reset my password**. The link expires after a limited time (normally 60 minutes).
4. Enter your email, a **New password** and **Confirm new password**, then click **Reset password**.

### Change your password

Open **Account & password** from the menu with your name, fill in **Current password**, **New password** and **Confirm new password**, and click **Change password**. You are signed out of your other devices.

Your name, email and mobile number on the account cannot be changed by you. If they are wrong, contact the Recruitment Section.

## 4. Start an application

1. On the **Advertisements** page, click **View details and apply** for the advertisement you want.
2. Read the **General instructions**, the details of each post (vacancies, age limit, application fee, essential and desirable qualifications), and any advertisement PDFs listed under **Advertisement documents**.
3. Under the post you want, click **Apply for this post**. If you are not signed in, you will be asked to sign in first.
4. The application form opens at Step 1.

Things to know:

- You can have only **one application per post**. If you already started one, you will see "You have already started an application for this post" and a link to **Go to My Applications**.
- There is a limit on how many unsubmitted drafts you may have at once. If you see "You have too many applications in progress", submit or discard one first.
- A post that shows **Not accepting applications** cannot be applied for.

## 5. Uploading documents

Each step shows a box called **Documents for this section** (or a similar title) with an **Upload** button for each document. Documents marked as required must be uploaded before you can submit.

Rules checked when you upload:

- **Allowed file types:** PDF, JPEG or PNG. Each document type says which of these it accepts and the maximum size, for example "PDF, up to 2 MB". The photograph accepts JPEG or PNG only.
- The file must not be empty and its contents must really be the type its name says.
- **PDFs that contain scripts, embedded files or launch actions are refused.** If this happens, open the document and use "Print to PDF" (or "Save as PDF") to make a plain PDF, then upload that.
- For qualifications, employment, merit certificates, training and memberships, you attach the certificate **against each row**. Save the step first; the message "Save the step to attach documents to this row" disappears once the row is saved.
- To change the photograph click **Replace**. To remove any other draft upload click the remove (bin) button next to it.
- Once you submit, your documents are locked and marked **submitted**. They cannot be removed afterwards.

## 6. Moving through the form

- The list on the left shows all 17 steps with a progress bar ("x / 17 complete"). On a phone, use the drop-down at the top.
- A step with a warning sign has items that still need attention. At the top of each step, **Still needed before you can submit:** lists exactly what is missing.
- **Save draft** saves the current step and keeps you on it. **Save & continue** saves and moves to the next step. **Back** returns to the previous step.
- If you try to leave a step with unsaved changes, the portal asks "Leave without saving them?".
- You can save a step that is incomplete only where the paper form allows it; otherwise the portal tells you which fields to correct.

## 7. Fill in the form, step by step

### Step 1 · Section 1 — Demand Draft / NEFT Details

1. The box at the top shows the **Fee payable for this application**, worked out from your category, gender, disability and ex-servicemen status as declared in later steps. If your details change, the fee may change.
2. If the fee is **Fee exempted**, just click **Save & continue** to confirm the exemption. Your eligibility for exemption is checked against your certificates later.
3. Otherwise choose **Mode of payment** (only the modes allowed by the advertisement are listed), and enter the **NEFT UTR / transaction number** or **DD number** (6 to 30 letters and digits), **Date of NEFT / DD**, **Amount (₹)** and **Name of the bank**.
4. The amount cannot be less than the fee payable.
5. Upload the fee receipt if asked.

Entering payment details does **not** mean the payment is verified. The Recruitment Section checks it against the Institute's bank records after you submit.

### Step 2 · Sections 2–4 — Advertisement and Post

The **Advertisement No.** is filled in for you. The **Date of application** is recorded automatically when you submit. You may change **4. Post applied for** only while the application is still a first draft; fee and document requirements may change with the post.

### Step 3 · Sections 5–16 — Personal Information

Fill in Sections 5 to 14 (name in CAPITAL LETTERS as in your Class 10 certificate, father's or husband's name, date and place of birth, mobile, email, nationality, gender, marital status, category, disability, nearest railway station), then **15. Address for correspondence** and **16. Permanent address** (tick **Same as the address for correspondence** if they are the same). Upload your photograph and other documents shown for this step. A category certificate is required for SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS where the advertisement asks for it.

### Step 4 · Section 17 — Ex-Servicemen

Answer YES or NO. If YES, give the **Ex-servicemen category** and details, and upload the certificate if asked.

### Step 5 · Section 18 — Educational, Professional and Technical Qualifications

Click **Add qualification** for each examination, **starting from Class 10 / Matriculation**. Class 10 must be included. Use the arrows to keep them in the order you passed. After saving, attach the certificate and mark sheets against each row.

### Step 6 · Section 19 — Employment History

1. Answer **Do you have any employment experience?** and, if YES, **Are you presently in service (Government / PSU / Autonomous Body / Private)?**
2. Click **Add employment** for each job. Tick **Currently working here** for your present job; otherwise enter the **To** date.
3. Rows are arranged most recent first when you save, and total experience is shown.
4. Your answer about being in service decides whether Section 32 (employer endorsement) applies to you.

### Step 7 · Section 20 — Criminal, Disciplinary and Vigilance Information

Answer YES or NO. If YES, give full details (court or authority, case number, charge, present status). You may upload a separate sheet.

### Steps 8–10 · Sections 21, 22 and 23

- **Section 21 — Certificates of Excellence and Merit**
- **Section 22 — Training, Consultancy and Professional Activities**
- **Section 23 — Corporate Membership of Professional Institutes**

For each, answer the YES / NO question. If YES, add a row for each item and attach supporting certificates.

### Step 11 · Section 24 — Suitability for the Post

Write a paragraph of **not more than 500 words**. A counter shows words remaining.

### Step 12 · Section 25 — Referees

1. Enter the exact number of referees the advertisement asks for: name, designation, organisation, postal address, email, phone, capacity in which they know you and years known.
2. For each, tick **The referee is a person resident in India** and **The referee is not my relation**. Both are required.
3. Each referee must have a **different email address**, and you cannot give your own email.
4. If you are in service, tick **The referee is my present / most recent employer or immediate superior** for one referee, **or** upload a testimonial from your employer in the box below.
5. Tick **I confirm the referee details are accurate…** and save.

Check the email addresses carefully: invitations are sent to them automatically after you submit.

### Step 13 · Sections 26–30 — Additional Declarations

Answer every question, 26 (a), 26 (b), 27, 28, 29 and 30. Details are required when you answer YES to 26 (a), 26 (b), 27 or 28, and a reason is required if you answer NO to 30 (ACRs / APARs).

### Step 14 · Section 31 — Other Achievements

Optional. Leave it blank if you have nothing to add, but **save the step anyway** to confirm you have reviewed it; otherwise submission is blocked.

### Step 15 · Declaration

Read the declaration, tick all four statements, enter **Place** and **Date** (within the last 30 days), and save. Saving records your electronic acceptance in place of a signature. If the Institute later updates the declaration text, you will be asked to accept it again before submitting.

### Step 16 · Section 32 — Employer Endorsement

- If you are not in service, or the advertisement does not call for it, the step says **Not applicable** and you may move on.
- If it says **Mandatory**, fill in the particulars from the endorsement issued by your forwarding officer, tick both certifications, and upload the signed and sealed endorsement.
- If it is optional for your post, you may still furnish it.

### Step 17 · Check List — Application Checklist

For every item mark **✓** (enclosed / uploaded), **X** (not enclosed) or **NA** (not applicable). Optionally add the page reference and choose the uploaded document. Any remaining required documents that belong to no other step are uploaded here under **Other documents**.

## 8. Submit your application

1. Click **Preview & submit**.
2. If anything is missing, the page lists **Items to complete before submission** with a **Fix** link for each.
3. Check the full preview. You can download a **Draft PDF**.
4. When the page says "All sections are complete", click **Submit application**.
5. Read the confirmation, tick the box and click **Submit application** again.

After submission:

- The application is locked and you get a **reference number**. Quote it in all correspondence.
- You receive an email with a PDF of your submitted application.
- Your referees are emailed a secure link.
- Applications cannot be submitted after the last date and time shown. Drafts that are not submitted by then cannot be submitted at all.

## 9. Download your receipt and application

From **My Applications** use the **Application** and **Receipt** buttons, or open **View status** and use **Acknowledgment receipt** and **Application PDF**. Every submitted version is listed under **Submitted versions** with its own **PDF** button.

## 10. Track your application

Open **My Applications** and click **View status**. You will see:

| Shown as | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** / "Draft — not submitted" | Not yet submitted. |
| **Submitted** / "Submitted — received" | Submitted; awaiting or received by the Recruitment Section. |
| "Under scrutiny" | The application is being checked. |
| "Clarification requested" | The Recruitment Section needs information from you. Respond before the due date. |
| "Scrutiny completed" | Scrutiny has finished. |
| "Under consideration by the Screening Committee" | Forwarded to the committee. |
| **Reopened** / "Reopened for correction" | You may correct and resubmit. |
| **Withdrawn** | You withdrew the application. |

**Application fee:** Not yet submitted · Awaiting verification · Verified · Not accepted — see remarks · Exempted.

**Employer endorsement:** Not applicable · Awaiting endorsement · Submitted — awaiting verification · Verified · Not accepted — see remarks.

**Referees** (per referee): Received · Awaiting · Not received (deadline passed) · Invitation withdrawn · Not yet invited. On the dashboard the overall referee column shows Not started · Awaiting recommendations · Some received · All received · Exception approved.

You may also get an email when the Recruitment Section records that your application has been received, and, if the Recruitment Section chooses to notify you, an email about the result of scrutiny. An acknowledgment confirms receipt only; it does not confirm eligibility.

## 11. Respond to a clarification request

1. You get an email "Clarification required" and a notification on My Applications.
2. Open the application status page and go to **Clarification requests**.
3. Type **Your response** (up to 5000 characters).
4. If uploads are allowed for that request, click **Add file** (up to 10 files, 10 MB each, PDF / JPEG / PNG) and add a description for each.
5. Click **Send response**. You receive an email confirming receipt.

Your submitted application is not changed by a response; it is recorded separately. After the due date you can no longer respond online.

## 12. Correct a reopened application

1. You get an email "Your application … has been reopened for correction" giving the reason and the date by which to resubmit.
2. Click **Continue** on My Applications. The form shows the reason and, if limited, "Only these steps can be edited". Other steps are read-only (shown with a lock).
3. Make the corrections. You can add corrected documents; documents already submitted stay on record.
4. Go to **Preview & submit** and click **Resubmit application**.
5. A new version is created. Earlier versions remain on file. If you changed a referee's email, a fresh invitation goes to the new address.

If the correction window closes before you resubmit, the form becomes read-only. Contact the Recruitment Section.

## 13. Withdraw an application

If the advertisement allows it, a **Withdraw…** button appears on the status page. Give an optional reason, tick the confirmation and click **Withdraw application**. **Withdrawal cannot be reversed.** Pending referee invitations are cancelled. Once scrutiny has reached a result or the application is with the Screening Committee, you can no longer withdraw online; contact the Recruitment Section.

## 14. Discard a draft

On My Applications, click **Discard** next to an unsubmitted draft and confirm with **Discard draft**. The draft and its uploads are deleted permanently.

---

## 15. Confidentiality of referee recommendations

**You will never see what your referees wrote.** Recommendations go directly to the Institute and are seen only by authorised staff and the Screening Committee. The portal shows you only whether each recommendation has been received. Please do not ask referees to send you copies through the portal; there is no such facility.

## 16. Troubleshooting and common questions

**I did not receive the verification email.** Check your spam or junk folder. Click **Resend email** on My Applications. Make sure you registered with the correct address; if not, contact the Recruitment Section.

**The verification link says it is invalid or expired.** Links are valid for 48 hours. Sign in and click **Resend verification email**.

**The password reset link does not work.** Use the newest email only, and use it within the time stated. Request a new link if needed.

**My upload was rejected.** Read the message: the file may be too large, the wrong type, empty, or a PDF with active content. Reduce the size (scan in black-and-white or lower resolution) or "Print to PDF" and try again.

**The Submit button is greyed out.** Either some items are incomplete (listed on the Preview page) or your email is not verified.

**The last date has passed.** A draft cannot be submitted after the closing date and time. The portal cannot accept late applications.

**"The fee payable has changed".** You changed your category or other details after entering the payment. Go back to Step 1 and update the payment details.

**My fee or endorsement shows "Not accepted — see remarks".** The Recruitment Section's remarks appear in red under the status on your application's status page. If you need to send anything in response, wait for a clarification request or contact the Recruitment Section quoting your reference number.

**A referee says they did not get the email.** Ask them to check their spam folder. The invitation is sent only after you submit. If the address was wrong, contact the Recruitment Section, who can correct it and send a replacement invitation.

**I made a mistake after submitting.** You cannot edit a submitted application. Contact the Recruitment Section; they may reopen it for correction.

**Who do I contact?** Use the contact email on the advertisement page, or the details on the **Privacy notice** page.
