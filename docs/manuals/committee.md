# Screening Committee Manual

**Non-Teaching Recruitment Portal, ABV-IIITM Gwalior**

This manual is for members of a Screening Committee, including the committee **Chair**, who evaluate applications forwarded to them after scrutiny.

---

## 1. Getting access

1. The Super Administrator creates your staff account with the **Screening Committee Member** role. You receive an email with a link to set your password. If it has expired, ask for a new password reset link.
2. The Recruitment Section adds you to a committee for a particular advertisement (and, sometimes, a particular post), and marks one member as **Chair**.
3. Sign in at the portal's **Sign in** page. You land on the **Screening Committee** page.

You see only applications that have been forwarded to a committee you belong to.

## 2. At a glance

1. The Recruitment Section forwards applications to your committee, normally only those **Recommended** at scrutiny.
2. Open each application and study the **Application**, **Documents**, **Referee recommendations** and **Scrutiny report**.
3. Depending on the committee's mode:
   - **Individual evaluations:** each member records and submits their own evaluation; the Chair then records the committee decision.
   - **Collective decision:** the Chair (or an authorised officer) records one evaluation and decision for the whole committee.
4. Use the **Comparison report** to see all candidates side by side.

The committee's work is **read-only** with respect to the application. You cannot change the application, its documents or the referee recommendations. You record only your own evaluation (and, if Chair, the decision).

---

## 3. The Screening Committee page

At the top, a card for each of your committees shows its name, advertisement and post, a **Chair** badge if you chair it, its mode (**Collective decision** or **Individual evaluations**) and the number of applications. Each card has **Applications** and **Comparison report** buttons.

Below, the **Applications** table lists forwarded applications. Use the drop-down to show **All my committees** or one committee. Columns:

- **Reference**, **Candidate** (with category), **Post / Advt.**
- **Scrutiny** — the scrutiny outcome
- **Referees** — overall referee status
- **Committee** — the committee's decision so far (Pending until recorded)
- **My evaluation** — Not started, Draft saved or Submitted

Click **Open** to work on an application.

## 4. Study an application

The workspace header shows the candidate, reference number, post, advertisement, your committee and mode ("You are the chair" if applicable), and the scrutiny and committee status. The tabs are:

- **Application** — the complete application as last submitted by the candidate.
- **Documents** — every uploaded document. Click **Open** or **Download**. If you see "Your role does not include opening documents.", contact the Recruitment Section.
- **Referee recommendations** — see section 7. This tab appears only if your role includes reading confidential recommendations (committee members have this by default).
- **Scrutiny report** — the **Status summary** (fee, documents, referees, endorsement, scrutiny, outcome), each **Stage report** with reasons, **Outstanding deficiencies**, the **Checklist verification** verdicts, **Eligibility (advisory)** checks, **Observations** and **Clarifications**.

The eligibility checks are computed from what the candidate declared and are advisory only; the scrutiny officers verified them against the documents.

## 5. Record your evaluation (Individual mode)

1. Open the application. The **My evaluation** box is on the right.
2. Answer each criterion. On the standard form these are:
   - **Eligibility assessment** — Eligible, Not eligible, or Doubtful — refer
   - **Essential qualification verified** — Yes / No
   - **Experience assessment** — 1 (Insufficient) to 5 (Outstanding)
   - **Referee feedback review** (optional)
   - **Observations** (optional)

   The Recruitment Section may have configured a different form for your committee.
3. Choose a **Recommendation**. On the standard form: **Recommended**, **Not Recommended** or **Requires Further Review**.
4. Add **Reasons / remarks**.
5. Click **Save draft** to keep your work and return later, or **Submit evaluation…** when ready. The submit button stays disabled while a required criterion is unanswered ("… required criterion/criteria unanswered").
6. Check the summary and click **Confirm and submit**.

**Once submitted, your evaluation is locked and cannot be changed.**

### Seeing other members' evaluations

The **Committee evaluations** box shows a count of submitted recommendations (for example "Recommended: 2"). The detailed assessments of other members become visible to you **only after you submit your own evaluation**. The Chair can see them at any time.

## 6. Record the committee decision (Chair)

The Chair records the decision. The Recruitment Section may also authorise another officer to record it.

1. Open the application. In **Individual** mode, review the members' evaluations first.
2. In the **Committee decision** box:
   - In **Collective** mode, first answer the evaluation criteria on behalf of the committee.
   - Choose a **Decision**: **Recommended**, **Not recommended** or **Requires further review**.
   - Enter **Reasons** (required).
3. Click **Record decision…**, check the summary and click **Confirm**.

The decision becomes the application's **Committee status**, and the screen shows "Recorded by [name], [date and time]".

To change it, click **Revise decision**, make the change and confirm. The earlier decision is superseded; the change, with the previous and new values, is recorded in the audit trail.

Members who cannot record the decision see "The decision is recorded by the committee chair."

## 7. Read referee recommendations

1. Open the **Referee recommendations** tab. It is marked "CONFIDENTIAL — completed referee recommendations" and lists each completed recommendation with the referee's name, reference number, date and overall recommendation. Below the list you see how many referees have responded.
2. Click **Open** to read one. The window shows the referee's details, the answers to each question, the **Overall recommendation**, any **Additional comments**, any **Recorded corrections** made by the Recruitment Section, the referee details as entered by the candidate, and an integrity hash.
3. Click **PDF** to download a copy.

Every time you open or download a recommendation, the portal records your name, the time and your network address.

## 8. Use the Comparison report

Click **Comparison report** (on your committee's card) or **Comparison** (inside an application). The table lists every application forwarded to the committee:

**Reference**, **Candidate**, **Category** (with a PwD tag), **Highest qualification**, **Experience**, **Scrutiny** outcome, **Referee recommendations** (each referee's overall recommendation — shown only if you are permitted to read confidential recommendations), **Member evaluations** (counts of submitted recommendations) and **Decision**.

Click a column heading such as Reference, Candidate, Experience or Decision to sort. Click a reference number to open the application. Click **Print** for a paper copy. The report states: "Referee recommendations are confidential. This report is for the use of the Screening Committee only."

## 9. Status glossary

| Item | Values |
|---|---|
| My evaluation | Not started · Draft saved · Submitted |
| Committee status / decision | Pending · Recommended · Not recommended · Requires further review |
| Scrutiny outcome | Recommended · Incomplete · Not recommended (Pending if not yet decided) |
| Referee status | Not started · Pending · Partial · Complete · Exception (the Recruitment Section waived the requirement) |
| Document verification | Pending · Verified · Rejected / Deficient |

## 10. Confidentiality

- **Referee recommendations are never disclosed to the candidate.** Do not discuss or share their contents outside the committee.
- Recommendations, documents and the comparison report are for the committee's use only. Printed copies should be handled and disposed of according to the Institute's rules.
- Every view or download of a recommendation or document is logged, and the Recruitment Section can review these logs.
- Your evaluations and decisions are recorded with your name and time, and changes to a decision are audited.

## 11. Troubleshooting

**"You are not a member of any Screening Committee."** The Recruitment Section has not yet added you to a committee.

**"No applications have been forwarded to your committees yet."** Wait for the Recruitment Section to forward applications.

**"This application is not assigned to your committee."** The application belongs to another committee. Use the link from your own queue.

**"Your evaluation has been submitted and can no longer be changed."** Submitted evaluations are locked. Add any further view in discussion with the Chair, who records the decision with reasons.

**"This committee records a collective decision; individual evaluations are not used."** Your committee is in Collective mode; only the Chair records the evaluation and decision.

**"Only the committee chair or an authorised officer records the committee decision."** Ask the Chair to record it.

**I cannot open documents, or the Referee recommendations tab is missing.** Your role lacks that permission. Contact the Recruitment Section.

**The scrutiny report will not load.** Reload the page. If the problem continues, contact the Recruitment Section.
