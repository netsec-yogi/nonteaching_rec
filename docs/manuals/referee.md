# Referee Manual

**Confidential Referee Recommendation, ABV-IIITM Gwalior Non-Teaching Recruitment**

This manual is for people who have been named as a referee by a candidate applying for a non-teaching post at ABV-IIITM Gwalior.

---

## 1. Who this is for and how you get access

- A candidate has given your name and email address as a referee in their application.
- When the candidate submits the application, the portal sends you an email titled **"Request for Confidential Referee Recommendation — ABV-IIITM Gwalior Non-Teaching Recruitment"**.
- The email contains a button **Submit Referee Recommendation**. This link is your only means of access.
- **You do not need to register or sign in.** The link is personal to you. Please do not forward it to anyone else.
- The email also gives the date by which you should submit.

## 2. At a glance

1. Open the link in the email.
2. Check the candidate's name, the post and the deadline.
3. If asked, confirm your email address with a 6-digit code.
4. Check or correct your own details (**Section A · Referee information**).
5. Answer the assessment questions (**Section B · Assessment of the applicant**), choose an **Overall recommendation**, and add any comments.
6. Click **Review my answers**, check them, tick the good-faith statement and click **Submit recommendation**.
7. Note the reference number shown on screen.

Complete the form in one sitting if you can (see [If you are interrupted](#6-if-you-are-interrupted)).

---

## 3. Open the recommendation form

1. Click **Submit Referee Recommendation** in the email, or copy the full link into your browser. Use the link exactly as it appears.
2. The page titled **Confidential Referee Recommendation** shows:
   - **Applicant** — the candidate's name
   - **Post applied for**
   - **Advertisement No.**
   - **Please submit by** — the deadline, with the number of days left
3. Read the introduction. It explains that your recommendation is confidential.

If you see a message instead of the form, see [What the messages mean](#7-what-the-messages-mean).

## 4. Confirm your email address (only if asked)

For some advertisements the Institute asks referees to confirm that they can receive email at the invited address. If you see **Confirm your email address**:

1. Click **Send verification code**.
2. A 6-digit code is emailed to the address the invitation was sent to. It is valid for 15 minutes.
3. Type the code in **Verification code** and click **Verify**.
4. If the code has expired or you entered it wrongly, click **Send a new code**. After several wrong attempts you must request a new code.

## 5. Fill in the recommendation

### Section A · Referee information

These details were entered by the candidate. Check them and correct them if necessary:

- **Name**, **Designation**, **Organisation / Institution**, **Email address** (all required)
- **Telephone / Mobile** (optional)
- **Relationship with the applicant** (required, for example supervisor, head of office, teacher)
- **Period for which you have known the applicant** (required, for example 6 years)
- **Nature of association** (optional)

Your corrections are recorded as you give them. The candidate's original entries are kept separately for the Institute's reference.

### Section B · Assessment of the applicant

Answer each question. Questions marked with an asterisk (*) are required. The Institute decides the questions; the standard form asks:

- In what capacity you know the applicant (choose one)
- How long you have known the applicant
- Ratings from **1 · Poor** to **5 · Excellent** for professional competence, integrity and character, reliability, ability to work independently, teamwork, and suitability for the advertised post
- **Would you recommend the applicant for the position?** (Yes / No)
- Any additional observations (optional)

Then:

1. Choose one **Overall recommendation**. On the standard form the options are Strongly Recommended, Recommended, Recommended with Reservations and Not Recommended.
2. Add **Additional comments** if you wish (optional, up to 10,000 characters).
3. A progress bar shows how many required answers are complete.
4. Click **Review my answers**. **Nothing is sent until you confirm on the next screen.**

If any required answer is missing, the message "Please complete the highlighted fields." appears and the fields are marked.

### Review and submit

1. The review screen shows everything you entered. Click **Edit** next to a section, or **Back**, to change anything.
2. Tick the good-faith statement ("I confirm that the information given above is accurate to the best of my knowledge and is submitted in good faith.").
3. Click **Submit recommendation**.
4. The screen shows **Recommendation received** with a **Reference number** and the date and time. Note the reference number for any correspondence. You may then close the window.

**Once submitted, the recommendation cannot be changed.** You also receive a confirmation email with the recommendation reference (it does not repeat what you wrote).

## 6. If you are interrupted

- The portal does **not** save a draft of your answers on the Institute's server.
- If you reload the page in the same browser tab, your unsaved answers are restored and the message "Your unsaved answers from this browser session have been restored." appears.
- If you close the browser or switch to another device, your answers may be lost and you will need to start again using the same link, as long as the deadline has not passed.

---

## 7. What the messages mean

| Message title | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This link is not valid** | The link is incomplete or was copied wrongly. | Open the link directly from the email. If it still fails, write to the Recruitment Section. |
| **The deadline has passed** | The deadline for this invitation is over. | Contact the Recruitment Section if you still wish to submit; they can extend the deadline, and the same link will then work again. |
| **This invitation has been withdrawn** | The Institute has cancelled this link, for example because a new invitation was issued. | Use the link in the most recent email from the Institute. |
| **Recommendation received** | A recommendation has already been submitted with this link. | Nothing further is needed. |
| "Too many attempts. Please wait a minute and try again" | Too many requests were made in a short time. | Wait a minute and try again. Your answers stay on the page. |

The Recruitment Section's contact email is shown at the bottom of the form and on each message page.

## 8. Reminders

If you have not yet submitted, the portal may send you reminder emails titled "Reminder: Confidential Referee Recommendation for …", using the same link. The number and timing of reminders are set by the Recruitment Section for each advertisement, and no more than one reminder is sent per day. If you have already submitted, please ignore any reminder.

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- **Your recommendation is never shown to the candidate.** The candidate can see only whether a recommendation has been received, not what it says.
- It is made available only to the authorised Recruitment Section staff and the Screening Committee, for this recruitment.
- Every time an authorised member of staff opens or downloads a recommendation, the portal records who did so and when.
- Your submission is stored together with a fingerprint of its contents so that any later change can be detected. If the Institute ever needs to correct a detail (for example a mistyped email address), the correction is recorded alongside your original, with the officer's name and reason; your original submission is not overwritten.

---

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**I did not receive the invitation email.** Invitations are sent only after the candidate submits the application. Check your spam or junk folder. If the candidate gave a wrong address, they should contact the Recruitment Section, who can send a replacement invitation to the correct address.

**Can I forward the link to a colleague to fill in?** No. The link is personal to you. If someone else should be the referee, the candidate must contact the Recruitment Section.

**The link has expired but I want to submit.** Write to the Recruitment Section at the contact address shown. If they extend the deadline, open the same link again.

**I submitted by mistake or want to change an answer.** A submitted recommendation cannot be changed online. Write to the Recruitment Section quoting the reference number.

**Can I upload a letter instead?** No. The form does not accept attachments. Use **Additional comments** for anything you wish to add.

**I got a new email with a different link.** Always use the most recent link. Earlier links stop working once a replacement is issued.
